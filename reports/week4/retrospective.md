# Sprint Retrospective — Sprint 2 (MVP v2)

## What went well
- Daily Scrum meetings helped the team stay in touch and track progress.
- Task distribution was clear. Everyone understood the responsibilities.
- Communication has improved during the sprint. The team members responded faster, and everyone was aware of each other's work status.
- Code reviews were not extremely fast, but they were consistent and did not become a major bottleneck.

## What did not go well
- Not all team members attended Daily Scrum regularly, which affected alignment.
- Some deadlines and agreements were not met, that's why some delays happened.
- Several tasks were underestimated, especially optimization work and fixing UI freezes.

## What changed compared to the previous Sprint
- Communication became more active and transparent.
- Team members started updating each other more frequently on task status.
- We tried to plan more realistically, though some tasks were still underestimated.

## Improvements for the next Sprint
1. Ensure full attendance at Daily Scrum to keep everyone aligned.
2. Split large or uncertain tasks into smaller, more accurately estimable pieces.
3. Start riskier work (e.g., performance optimization) earlier in the sprint.
