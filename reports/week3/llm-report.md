All the participants used AI to analyze the assignment, understand what was happening in each section, divide tasks, clarify unclear phrases, and explain the intricacies of certain requirements. And also members used AI for educational purposes and automation of some processes (in order to speed up work): we used AI to formulate the acceptance criteria and Definition of Done, to learn new material about writing websites, to understand and fix mistakes in writing in Streamlit and PostgreSQL code for the backend, to help us work with Git/GitHub workflow and OpenCV-related implementation questions, to check the work for compliance with the requirements and an appropriate explanation, to create subtitles for audio recordings (raw and slightly low-quality audio transcription without analysis and with restrictions).
AI was not used in other parts of this assignment. No one used AI to generate submitted content for the assignment; AI was viewed as a supporting tool.
